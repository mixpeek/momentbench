Methodology

full spec in the technical report
Corpus

75 hours across three verticals: government proceedings, aerospace & field operations, and archival film. Every asset is public domain or CC BY, with a machine-generated attribution manifest.

Query tiers

T1 single-condition visual · T2 cross-modal joint conditions · T3 corpus-scale with explicit hard negatives · T4 temporal reasoning. 500 queries, two annotators each, adjudicated gold labels.

Scoring

A hit requires the correct asset and temporal IoU ≥ 0.5 against the gold in/out points. We report Recall@1, Recall@5, and mean temporal IoU per tier.

Reproducibility

The harness, public query set, and model adapters are open source. A 150-query private held-out set guards against contamination; labs can evaluate against it via a hosted scoring endpoint.
