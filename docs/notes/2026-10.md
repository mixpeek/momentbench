# MomentBench results note: October 2026

Release 0.2.5 came out on 2026-10-17, and the leaderboard has not changed since 2026-10-18.
Every score below comes from
[`docs/leaderboard.json` at commit `6b9166e`](https://github.com/mixpeek/momentbench/blob/6b9166e/docs/leaderboard.json),
generated at `a13fb3d`. Positive-query counts come from the scored query files.

Rows compare only within a group. These are the three primary groups: agentic track,
anonymized titles, standard prompt, one trial per query for each model and harness, on the
scored query sets. The metric is positive-query Recall@1 with its 95% interval. A hit is a first submitted
moment in a gold video with temporal IoU of at least 0.5 against a gold span. The
compatibility keys are `05841b8cc16d0b1b` (aerospace), `689ea1623b054aa6` (film) and
`39a268172b68fe33` (robotics).

## How to read them

- The individual intervals for claude-opus-5-5 and gemini-3.8-flash overlap in aerospace
  and robotics. This note does not report a paired comparison.
- Trial outcomes differ by row. gemini-3.8-flash hit the step limit on 55 of 154 aerospace
  queries and 16 of 49 robotics queries. gpt-5.4-mini returned no submission on 24 and 9.
- Film has no non-Claude row in its primary group.
- claude-haiku-5-5 appears twice in aerospace and robotics because it ran under both
  harnesses.
- With one trial per query, the intervals cover query sampling and leave out variation
  between runs and uncertainty about new source videos.
- The labels are model-adjudicated, without human review.

No-answer accuracy and net score are exploratory: the corpus-wide absence screen recovered
12 of 26 known positives, as recorded in [`docs/labels.md`](../labels.md).

The abstain-prompt groups and the prediction-file baselines are in
the same file.
