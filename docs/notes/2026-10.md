## How to read them

- The intervals for claude-opus-5-5 and gemini-3.8-flash overlap in both aerospace and
  robotics, so these runs do not separate the two.
- Trial outcomes differ by row. gemini-3.8-flash hit the step limit on 55 of 154 aerospace
  queries and 16 of 49 robotics queries. gpt-5.4-mini returned no submission on 24 and 9.
- Film has no non-Claude row in its primary group.
- claude-haiku-5-5 appears twice in aerospace and robotics because it ran under both
  harnesses.
- Each row is one trial, so the intervals cover query sampling and leave out variation
  between runs.

No-answer accuracy and net score are exploratory: the corpus-wide absence screen recovered
12 of 26 known positives, as recorded in [`docs/labels.md`](../labels.md).

The abstain-prompt groups and the prediction-file baselines are in
[`docs/leaderboard.json`](../leaderboard.json).
