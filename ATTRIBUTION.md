## Excluded License Types

The following license types are **not** included in MomentBench:

- CC BY-NC (no commercial use)
- CC BY-ND (no derivatives)
- CC BY-NC-SA
- Broadcast content (NFL, NBA, EPL, etc.)
- MIT OCW / university lectures (typically CC BY-NC-SA)
- Corporate webcasts (copyright held by issuer)
